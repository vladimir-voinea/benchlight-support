Benchlight

Privacy

Short version: no account, no tracking, no advertising, and anonymous usage data that stays off until you are asked.

Anonymous usage data — off until you are asked

The first time you open the bench it asks once whether it may share anonymous usage. Until you say yes, nothing is recorded and nothing is sent. Your answer is kept in the Keychain, so a reinstall does not ask again, and you can change it at any time in About.

When it is on, what is sent is: which levels you open, when you solve one or leave one, how many runs it took, how long it took, whether you used the tutor, and which handbook pages you read. Along with that: the app version, the iOS version and the device model. Nothing else.

What is never collected

No name, no email, no account, no advertising identifier, no location, no contacts, no free text, no screenshots, no per-tap trail, and nothing that identifies you as a person. The app does no tracking across other apps or websites and shows no advertising.

How uploads are proved

Every upload is signed by Apple's App Attest, so the collector can tell a real copy of Benchlight from a script. The device identifier that goes with it is a salted hash made on your phone — not your IDFV in the clear, and not shared with any other app.

What stays on your device

Your solved levels and the level you last had open live in two small files on the phone. They leave only if you choose Export, which writes a file wherever you pick.

Turning it off

About → Anonymous usage data. Turning it off stops recording, deletes anything already queued on the device, and stops all sending.

Purchases

The app is bought once from the App Store. Apple handles the transaction; the developer receives no personal information about you.

Children

No account, no chat, no user-generated content, no advertising, and no collection beyond the anonymous usage described above — which is off unless a person turns it on.
