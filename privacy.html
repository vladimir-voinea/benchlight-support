Privacy

Short version: Benchlight collects nothing, because it cannot reach anything.

No data collected

The app contains no networking code, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no crash reporter. It asks for no permissions and makes no network requests. The App Store privacy label therefore reads “No, we do not collect data from this app”.

What stays on your device

Two small files: your solved levels and the level you last had open. They never leave the phone unless you choose Export, which writes a file to a place you pick.

Purchases

The app is bought once from the App Store. Apple handles the transaction; the developer receives no personal information about you.

Children

There is no account, no chat, no user-generated content and no data collection of any kind.
