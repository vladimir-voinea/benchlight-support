Benchlight

Privacy

Short version: nothing leaves this phone until you are asked and say yes, and the switch that turns it off again is in the app’s About screen.

Off until you say otherwise

Benchlight asks once, the first time you open it, whether it may share anonymous usage data. It is off by default. If you say no, the app never sends anything, and it does not ask again. You can change your answer at any time in About.

What is sent, when you say yes

  • which levels you open, and which ones you leave unsolved;
  • how long a level took you, and how many runs it took;
  • whether you used the tutor or the handbook;
  • a random identifier created on your device the first time the app runs, so the same install is not counted twice;
  • the app version, the iOS version, and the device model (for example “iPhone”).

It goes to a server run by the developer, not to any advertising or analytics company. Every batch is signed with App Attest, which proves the events came from a real copy of Benchlight and cannot be faked.

What is never sent

Your name, your email, your Apple ID, your location, your contacts, your photos, your advertising identifier. Benchlight has no account system, so it could not tie any of that to you even if it wanted to. The identifier above is not linked to you and is never used to follow you across other apps or websites.

What stays on your phone

Your progress — which levels you have solved and the one you last had open. That lives on the device and nowhere else. Deleting the app wipes it; Export writes a file to a place you choose, and nothing else can read it.

Turning it off

The switch in About stops the app sending anything from that moment, and deletes any events still waiting on the device. What was already sent cannot be pulled back, because it was never linked to you.

Purchases

The app is bought once from the App Store. Apple handles the transaction; the developer receives no personal information about you.

Children

There is no account, no chat, no user-generated content, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.
