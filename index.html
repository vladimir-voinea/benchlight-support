Benchlight: Physics Puzzles is an offline iPhone game where you solve illustrated physics problems by doing the calculation.

Nothing to sign in to

The app has no account, no server and no network access. Your progress lives on your device.

Progress and reinstalling

Solved levels are stored on the device. Use Progress → Export in the app to keep a copy before deleting it; reinstalling starts clean unless you import that file.

A level that will not solve

Every level’s answer is solved for and proved before it ships. If a number that you can defend is refused, send the level name and the number and it will be checked.

The apparatus is fictional

Nothing in the app measures the real world, and it is not advice for a real laboratory, workshop or classroom demonstration.
